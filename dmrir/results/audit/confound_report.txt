Interpretation guide: if every frame's ROI is stretched to the same min/max (roi_max ~255,
many pixels at max, similar across patients), the images are display-scaled and absolute
intensity is NOT temperature. If roi_max or n_unique_levels differ strongly by class, the
classes may have been exported with different scaling/windowing, which a classifier can
exploit without any physiological signal. This check cannot prove which case applies;
the dataset documentation / camera export settings are the authority.
